How it works

  1. 01

    Point it at a folder

    Watch Downloads or any folder, and pick where sorted files should live.

  2. 02

    Tell it your subjects

    Load the B.Tech Electrical preset or paste your syllabus. Keywords match filenames and document text.

  3. 03

    Review, then move

    See the folder tree first. Confirm what moves, and revert the whole batch if you change your mind.
